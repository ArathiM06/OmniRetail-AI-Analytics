PART 6   IMPORTANT MODEL SETUP
STEP 16   Avoid Giving the Model the Answer
Do not simply use Recency to predict Churn_Status when Churn_Status was created directly from Recency.
That would give the model the answer in advance.
	

Instead, ideally use:
Historical customer behaviour  →  Future churn outcome
For a proper predictive setup:
* Use an earlier period to calculate customer behaviour.
* Use a later period to determine whether the customer actually churned.
